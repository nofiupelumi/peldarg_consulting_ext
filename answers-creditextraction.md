Since you chose both HMAC and bearer token, I would implement:

Required headers

Authorization: Bearer <RESULT_UPLOAD_TOKEN>

X-Peldarg-Signature: sha256=<hmac>

Verification flow

verify bearer token

verify HMAC against raw body using CALLBACK_HMAC_SECRET

verify idempotency key not previously processed

only then finalize callback

That gives you layered protection.
